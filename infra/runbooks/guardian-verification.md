# Guardian relationship verification

Owner decision, 18 October 2026: record guardian and student acknowledgment, review an uploaded school marksheet/performance-card image naming the parent/guardian, and use a staff video/phone review when the named relationship is missing or does not match. This is a human evidence-review workflow, not OCR or a qualified identity-provider integration. Staff separately checks adult status and authority: a school report, email possession or an elder-sibling declaration does not establish adulthood or legal authority by itself. An elder sibling must be an authorized adult before acting as a guardian.

## Restricted intake and review

A signed-in guardian declares their name, the learner name, relationship and learner age band; chooses a proposed management mode; and permits evidence processing under `guardian-verification-v1`. Only the restricted verification request is created at this stage. This permission is separate from `tuition-and-learning-records` consent, which is still required after approval before creating the learner profile or starting enquiries.

Use the student's separate email account for in-platform acknowledgment. The student sees the guardian's name, learner name and proposed management mode before explicitly acknowledging. For learners without an email account, staff records the acknowledgment during a video/phone review. Do not invent an independent child's consent or pretend a guardian clicked on the child's behalf; guardian data consent and the child's relationship acknowledgment are distinct. If acknowledgment or authority cannot be established, request changes or reject the request rather than approving it.

The guardian uploads a PNG/JPEG, at most5 MiB. Encourage cropping/redaction of unrelated grades, other pupils and identifiers while retaining the names/school context needed for review. Files are private, outside the public web root and quarantined. The existing scanner has an8-second socket timeout; unavailable/infected/tampered files cannot be approved or downloaded. Retry/recovery uses the existing outbox and exact idempotency keys. Development EICAR signatures are not production malware qualification.

An MFA-authenticated ADMIN or region-scoped REVIEWER reviews the evidence and records a protected review reference and user-facing reason. Never put raw identity numbers, report contents or credentials in references, audit reasons or logs. The reviewer must independently attest adult status/authority. Approval also requires either the declared matching guardian name or a completed video/phone review, and the student's recorded acknowledgment (or staff-observed acknowledgment during that call). No self-review is allowed. A failed/uncertain write keeps its exact key; refresh authoritative state before reviewing a newer version. Changes requested allow an explicit revision, which resets the student acknowledgment.

Approval authorizes only the named learner proof. After separate learning-data consent, creating the matching learner consumes that proof once and links the acknowledged student account where present. A different/new learner needs a separate review. Proof consumption remains permanent even after replacement; approved declarations cannot be rewritten. Existing development synthetic fixtures retain their explicitly development-only setup path; production startup stays blocked. Revoking a proof pauses the guardian's verified state and invalidates the newly linked student's authorization. For recovery, use a new staff-call review if the old student authorization is paused, then attach its approved proof through POST /children/:id/guardian-verifications. The route checks current consent, ownership, name, version and existing student identity without replacing history. Do not use database edits.

## Lifecycle and delivery boundaries

Guardian and staff lists are cursor-paginated; ownership/region/status filtering precedes limits. Images require an authorized clean-file download and a fresh integrity check, with no-store/attachment/nosniff headers. Student acknowledgment pages expose no image, private staff reference or other learner history. Closed requests and feedback remain visible for recovery; file retention/deletion follows the approved privacy workflow, not arbitrary operator deletion.

Account/learner erasure scrubs request names/email/references, deletes cached mutation results containing that request and schedules physical image deletion. Deletion-ledger replay repeats that workflow. Review audit is retained under the explicitly recorded privacy-review retention exception; external storage/backups, approved retention periods and production deletion replay remain B14/release qualification.

B1 records the guardian's three proposed management modes (`GUARDIAN_ONLY`, `SHARED`, `STUDENT_LED`). L3 implements the corresponding runtime permissions next. Do not promise broader student permissions before that increment. Live email delivery, trained/named human reviewers, protected review-record custody, final notice/evidence policy and real-person acceptance are release requirements. No live charge, merge or deployment is enabled here.
