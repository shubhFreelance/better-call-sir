# B13 service follow-ups

This staff workflow supports teacher discovery/hiring, not individual-class administration. Thirty-day tasks are derived from authoritative teaching starts: Placement.startedAt for guardian-owned arrangements (including preserved legacy starts), and mutually confirmed TuitionMinimum.commencementDate for independent-adult arrangements. An unconfirmed hire never becomes a follow-up. No worker or email-delivery success is needed to discover due work; historical due starts remain reachable after downtime. Closed arrangements remain eligible so staff can record the actual outcome.

Five documented APIs provide scoped cursor queues, contact-history detail, reviewed self-claim/release and reviewed contact recording. Administrators see all eligible regions; operators see their own. Recent five-minute MFA, current role/region/auth epoch/session, guardian consent/proof, independent owner/profile, pending deletion and permanent deletion markers apply. Mutation retries recheck current authority before cached receipts. Claims and versioned contacts serialize per arrangement; operators cannot steal another staff member's task. Administrators may release an obsolete assignment. Contact requires the current assignee. Queue/history projections contain no addresses, contact credentials or free-form academic/child/staff notes. The current assignee alone may explicitly reveal the owner’s stored email/phone through a separately audited, fresh-authority private read. Release, privacy pause and revoked access deny subsequent reveals. The UI does not retain an inactive contact-details query or show cached details during revalidation.

A NO_RESPONSE attempt schedules a timestamp within the next thirty days and remains pending. CONTINUING and ENDED complete the follow-up only; neither changes the arrangement, commercial terms or immutable continuation statistics. HELP_REQUESTED completes the follow-up and atomically opens one participant-owned GENERAL/SAFETY/REPLACEMENT case, linked to the guardian placement or independent enquiry, with the recording staff member assigned. The existing case/replacement/financial-review workflow handles the problem. Duplicate-safe retries do not create another case or contact. No email/phone/video call is performed by these endpoints: staff explicitly attest a real contact or attempted contact outside the platform. Existing support access remains available before a thirty-day task is due.

Queue filtering (due/all pending, status, mine/unassigned) precedes cursor limits. Cursors bind kind, arrangement and current filter; disappeared/changed anchors fail closed and can restart at the first page. Contact history is separately paginated. Immutable metadata records actor, method, outcome, timestamp, retry timestamp and linked case only. Current privacy pause/erasure hides the task, including old mutation retries; restricted event/audit IDs stay within the existing operational retention-review boundary. Approved production retention, actual staffing/contact procedures, independent security/device review and operational acceptance remain launch qualification.

The stored ContinuationObservation is displayed as existing evidence (including unknown or missing), never rewritten or inferred from a staff phone response. Independent tasks explicitly have no observation until that reporting domain supports one; staff contact completion is not a cohort continuation metric. The due list is oldest-first; scheduling time is UTC and is displayed in Asia/Kolkata, using English/Hindi dates.
