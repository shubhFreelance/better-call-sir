# T2 minimum tuition

The owner-approved hiring policy is a fixed minimum, not a class-booking rule or an additional replacement fee. New guardian and independent-adult arrangements freeze the teacher's explicit 0/15/30-day preference and monthly amount when terms are opened. Fifteen days is half a month (rounding an odd paise upward); thirty is one month; zero is zero. Unspecified legacy preferences create no financial obligation and cannot be backfilled through these APIs.

Both learning acceptances precede financial commencement. The guardian owning a managed learner, or the independent adult owner, and the teacher each confirm the same actual calendar date. A sole pending confirmation can be withdrawn to correct its date. Once both confirm, the date and original commercial snapshot are immutable. Individual classes and recurring timetables are unnecessary. Learning delegation and optional adult supporters do not grant financial authority. Current consent, learner proof, session and teacher screening checks still apply.

`GET /tuition-minimums/{kind}/{id}` returns the frozen terms, two start confirmations, adjusted obligation, confirmed credits, outstanding amount and separately paginated credit/review histories. `kind` is PLACEMENT or INDEPENDENT. The period ends after the agreed number of calendar days from commencement; the end date does not erase unpaid minimum tuition. This ledger covers only the minimum, not later monthly tuition or per-class billing.

Either financial party can propose the portion of an already-paid amount credited toward the original minimum. Prepayments are allowed. Use a non-sensitive receipt label, never bank credentials or payment-account details. The other financial party confirms or rejects it. Confirmed credits cannot exceed the original minimum. Unique receipt labels, serialized version checks and keyed retries prevent accidental duplication; this is a mutually attested manual ledger, not independent bank verification. Credits remain recordable after closure. Platform-fee invoices and provider payments remain separate.

Ordinary closure and replacement preserve `max(0, adjusted minimum - confirmed credits)` after mutually confirmed commencement. Before commencement there is no minimum debt. Closing, seeking help or asking for replacement never requires payment through the platform. No replacement fee or automatic ordinary refund is added.

Any participant, including a managed learner or active adult supporter, may request a SAFETY or NON_DELIVERY assessment linked to an existing support case. Pending assessments mark the balance as disputed and block new financial commencement; help and replacement remain available. Regional independent staff with recent MFA review current terms and credits, then retain or reduce the minimum with a participant-visible reason. The linked case cannot be resolved through generic triage first. An excess of confirmed credits over the adjusted obligation is shown as an amount requiring refund assessment, never as an executed refund. No live collection or provider refund is triggered.

Amounts, receipt labels, decisions and actor identifiers are restricted financial/safety records retained with arrangements under the existing staff-approved financial/audit retention exception. Account deletion removes identity/profile data and revokes access; it does not silently cancel or rewrite retained financial evidence. Production retention periods, legal notices, real payment verification and provider/operational qualification remain launch requirements. No deployment is authorized here.
