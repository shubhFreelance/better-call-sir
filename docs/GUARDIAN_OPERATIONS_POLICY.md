## Guardian review procedure

1. Intake: explain the evidence purpose, who can review it, required names/context, proposed retention and privacy-request route. Collect only guardian name, named learner, relationship, age band, management choice and the chosen acknowledgment route. Use a separate case per learner. Do not request Aadhaar, bank details, full birth dates, unrelated school grades or other children's information through free-text fields.
2. Image: request one cropped/redacted school report showing learner/parent/guardian names and sufficient school context. PNG/JPEG up to5MiB. Upload only through the private application form; never accept evidence in personal email/chat. Quarantine, clean scan and integrity checks precede download or approval. Scanner failure means hold, never approve manually around it.
3. Separate acknowledgment: the student sees the named guardian and management choice, then acknowledges/refuses from their own account. If no student email is available, a staff-observed video/phone route can record acknowledgment. Never ask the guardian to click for the student. For a very young learner unable to give meaningful acknowledgment, hold and escalate the documented lawful-authority assessment; never fabricate a child response.
4. Authority: a matching report name, a video/phone call or email possession alone does not prove adulthood or lawful authority. Staff assesses adult status and the claimed authority separately using the approved human procedure. An elder sibling must be an authorized adult. Unclear or conflicting relationships remain on hold/request changes; do not collect additional identity-document categories before that procedure and purpose are approved.
5. Missing name: use the recorded video/phone fallback; record method/date/outcome and a protected review reference. A remote call is a review method, not an automatic identity-provider guarantee. Do not record calls or take additional photos by default; any future recording needs an approved purpose, access and retention policy.
6. Decision: current region-scoped REVIEWER/ADMIN with fresh MFA inspects the actual current revision, records adult/authority checks, document match/call result, acknowledgment and participant-safe reason. No self-review. Use the existing versioned decision API; inspect state and retry the same key after an uncertain result. Do not change the database directly.
7. After approval: proof applies only to this named learner and is consumed once when the matching profile is created. Ask for separate learning-data consent before intake. Review the guardian-only/shared/student-led mode and explain it to the student; no repeated guardian approval for each subject where the chosen mode permits the student's decision.
8. Refusal/changes/appeal: give a clear, non-accusatory reason and next action without exposing private review references. Revision resets acknowledgment. Escalate conflicts to the independent lead; no arbitrary support override or emailed password/code request. Recovery uses a new approved proof and the owned restoration endpoint; the old proof remains consumed.
9. Revocation: use the reviewed API, inspect the learner and current access, and confirm student authentication/refresh, new sharing and learning decisions stop. Preserve owner privacy correction/unsharing and lawful financial/safety history. Review every affected learner; another child's valid proof does not restore the revoked proof.

## Proposed evidence and record retention

No automatic time-based retention worker or production backup lifecycle is claimed implemented by this schedule. A scheduled purge with auditable receipts and exception handling must be qualified under B14 before real sensitive uploads. Do not silently turn these proposals into actual deletion rules or overwrite existing financial/safety retention exceptions.

| Data | Proposed normal limit | Exception / disposal |
|---|---|---|
| Unsubmitted verification draft and image |90 days after last activity | Close stale intake, then delete image/content; no renewed retention merely because an operator read it |
| Superseded report images |30 days after replacement of the image | Short appeal/review window; accessible only through the original authorized private route |
| Approved/rejected/cancelled/revoked report image |30 days after final decision | Approved proof metadata can persist without retaining the whole school report; approved appeal/hold must state purpose, owner and review date |
| Relationship decision metadata and consent/acknowledgment proof | While linked learner is active, then12 months after closure/erasure | Minimize retained identifiers; staff/privacy review must approve any longer lawful exception |
| Ordinary support-case content |12 months after case closure | Remove unnecessary academic/identity content; earlier correction/deletion through privacy review |
| Safeguarding incident evidence |12 months after case closure, subject to lawful preservation needs | Restricted hold with independent owner and90-day review; legal/safeguarding reviewer decides any longer requirement |
| Tuition terms, minimum ledger, credits and commercial dispute decisions |36 months after arrangement closure, subject to applicable legal/tax duties | Proposal requires professional review; no raw bank credentials; erasure must not silently rewrite retained obligations |
| Account/education/notes | Account purpose remains active; reviewed erasure on request or approved inactivity closure | No assumption that learning-consent withdrawal erases retained financial/safety evidence; stop prohibited use/sharing immediately |
| Encrypted external backup copies |30-day rolling window | Proposed infrastructure target; restore quarantined, replay deletion ledger before service/network access; never restore erased accounts to active access |
| Deletion markers and security audit | Enough to prove fulfillment and replay every retained backup; approve a specific minimized schedule in B14 | Avoid raw image/name content in markers/audit; independently document any lawful hold |

Proposed deletion service target: remove approved live-store image/content within7 days of a reviewed deletion decision; backup expiry within the approved rolling window. These targets must be accepted by the actual staff/storage operator before being published. The privacy owner must approve the final data categories, periods, legal basis, notices, hold procedure and response deadlines; proposed numbers are not represented as a statutory requirement.

For every purge, record case/object reference, reason, approval/hold reference, requested/completed times and storage/backup-deletion evidence. Retain only the minimum deletion evidence. The real storage operator must demonstrate orphan/superseded image deletion, retry after unavailable storage, a failed scan, denied download, permanent erasure and restored-backup replay. Current local files/test-signature scanner are development infrastructure.

## Safeguarding, contact and recovery

- Help and replacement must remain reachable without payment, waiting for a dispute outcome or completing a class. Never tell a learner to remain in an unsafe arrangement to satisfy the minimum.
- Triage safety reports separately from ordinary fit/preferences. Limit visibility to authorized regional staff and the safeguarding lead. Do not put sensitive allegations in notification subjects, contact-history outcomes or public teacher cards. Preserve minimal necessary evidence under a documented hold; prohibit retaliation.
- Clearly say the platform is not an emergency service. Where there is immediate danger, advise contacting local emergency services and a trusted safe adult; for the current India pilot,112 is the emergency number. Do not promise staff attendance or real-time monitoring without appointed coverage.
- Use platform-owned accounts/channels and permitted contacts only. No unapproved direct outreach to a child, private recording or export of school reports. A supporter is not automatically a lawful guardian; ADVICE and MANAGE scopes do not transfer account ownership, privacy, devices or financial authority.
- Same-mailbox OTP recovery restores the existing account after normal limits/expiry checks. Staff never asks for an OTP, password, recovery code or private key. Loss-of-mailbox identity claims need the separately approved independent human recovery procedure; no informal account reassignment. Staff MFA recovery uses the implemented two-person current-version process.
- Suspend/pause use through authorized workflows where appropriate. Safety/non-delivery minimum-tuition adjustments need independent regional MFA staff assessment and participant-visible reasons; generic support triage cannot silently resolve the linked financial dispute.

### A. Verification evidence permission / सत्यापन प्रमाण की अनुमति

**English:** We need to review your relationship with this named learner and your authority to act as their guardian. Upload a cropped school marksheet or performance card showing the relevant learner and parent/guardian names. Hide unrelated grades, identity numbers and other children's details. Authorized reviewers can view the private image after its safety scan. If the guardian name is missing or unclear, staff will request a phone/video review. A school-report name or a call alone does not verify adulthood or lawful authority. The student will separately acknowledge the relationship, or staff will record the approved acknowledgment route. Verification permission does not grant learning-data consent or hire a teacher. You can request correction/deletion through Account privacy. The proposed limit is30 days after the final decision for report images and90 days of inactivity for unused drafts, subject to a documented lawful hold. Other necessary decision records follow the reviewed retention schedule.

**हिन्दी:** हमें इस विद्यार्थी के साथ आपके रिश्ते और अभिभावक के रूप में आपकी अनुमति की समीक्षा करनी है। स्कूल की अंकतालिका या प्रगति रिपोर्ट का केवल वह हिस्सा अपलोड करें जिसमें विद्यार्थी और माता-पिता/अभिभावक के नाम हों। अनावश्यक अंक, पहचान नंबर और दूसरे बच्चों की जानकारी छिपाएँ। सुरक्षा जाँच के बाद केवल अधिकृत समीक्षक निजी तस्वीर देख सकते हैं। नाम न होने या स्पष्ट न होने पर कर्मचारी फ़ोन/वीडियो समीक्षा माँगेंगे। रिपोर्ट में नाम या केवल कॉल से वयस्क होने और वैध अधिकार की पुष्टि नहीं होती। विद्यार्थी रिश्ते की अलग पुष्टि करेगा, या कर्मचारी स्वीकृत तरीके से पुष्टि दर्ज करेंगे। यह अनुमति पढ़ाई के डेटा की सहमति या शिक्षक की नियुक्ति नहीं है। खाते की गोपनीयता से सुधार/मिटाने का अनुरोध कर सकते हैं। प्रस्तावित नीति में अंतिम निर्णय के30 दिन बाद रिपोर्ट की तस्वीर मिटाई जाएगी;90 दिन तक इस्तेमाल न हुए ड्राफ़्ट बंद किए जाएँगे। किसी वैध रोक को अलग दर्ज करना होगा। ज़रूरी निर्णय रिकॉर्ड समीक्षा की गई अवधि के अनुसार रहेंगे।

### B. Learning-data consent / पढ़ाई के डेटा की सहमति

**English:** After relationship approval, we use this learner's education, subject needs, teaching format, locality, budget and availability to prepare teacher discovery, reviewed enquiries and the tuition arrangement. These details are private and shared only with authorized participants and staff for the stated purpose. Choose guardian-only, shared or student-led learning; the learner sees the current choice. This does not transfer account ownership, device administration or financial authority. You can change permitted learning management and withdraw learning consent; new learning processing/sharing stops, while reviewed financial/safety records may be retained under the approved policy. Evidence permission and optional note sharing remain separate.

**हिन्दी:** रिश्ते की मंज़ूरी के बाद शिक्षक खोजने, पूछताछ की समीक्षा और ट्यूशन की व्यवस्था के लिए विद्यार्थी की पढ़ाई, विषय, पढ़ाने का तरीका, इलाका, बजट और उपलब्ध समय इस्तेमाल होते हैं। जानकारी निजी है और बताए गए उद्देश्य के लिए केवल अधिकृत लोगों तथा कर्मचारियों को मिलती है। केवल अभिभावक, साझा प्रबंधन या विद्यार्थी की अगुवाई चुनें; विद्यार्थी मौजूदा विकल्प देखेगा। इससे खाते का स्वामित्व, डिवाइस प्रबंधन या पैसों का अधिकार नहीं मिलता। आप अनुमत प्रबंधन बदल सकते हैं और पढ़ाई की सहमति वापस ले सकते हैं। नया उपयोग/साझा करना रुकता है; स्वीकृत नीति के अनुसार वित्तीय/सुरक्षा रिकॉर्ड रखे जा सकते हैं। प्रमाण की अनुमति और नोट साझा करने का निर्णय अलग हैं।

### C. Adult learner and optional supporter / वयस्क विद्यार्थी और वैकल्पिक मददगार

**English:** Independent signup requires an18+ declaration and your own permission to store your education for your private profile and teacher enquiries. Readiness and age are separate; the declaration is not verified age. Under18 learners use guardian-managed authorization. You may invite an adult supporter for read-only advice or learning-management help and revoke access. This does not make them your legal guardian or give them your login, devices, owner privacy requests or financial authority. Teacher contact email is shared only after both hiring parties confirm the specific terms.

**हिन्दी:** स्वतंत्र खाते के लिए18 वर्ष या अधिक की घोषणा और निजी प्रोफ़ाइल/शिक्षक पूछताछ के उद्देश्य से पढ़ाई की जानकारी रखने की आपकी सहमति चाहिए। तैयारी और उम्र अलग हैं; घोषणा उम्र का सत्यापन नहीं है।18 वर्ष से कम के विद्यार्थी अभिभावक की अनुमति से जुड़ते हैं। किसी वयस्क मददगार को केवल सलाह या पढ़ाई के प्रबंधन के लिए आमंत्रित कर सकते हैं और पहुँच वापस ले सकते हैं। वह कानूनी अभिभावक नहीं बनता और उसे आपका लॉगिन, डिवाइस, खाते की गोपनीयता या पैसों का अधिकार नहीं मिलता। शिक्षक और विद्यार्थी का संपर्क ईमेल तय शर्तों पर दोनों की पुष्टि के बाद ही साझा होता है।

### D. Tuition, ordinary replacement and safety exceptions / फ़ीस, बदलाव और सुरक्षा के अपवाद

**English:** Review the named teacher, subject, agreed monthly tuition and0/15/30-day minimum before accepting. A demo is optional. Fifteen days equals half the agreed monthly tuition; thirty equals one month. The minimum starts only when the financial parties both confirm the same actual teaching commencement date. Ordinary early exit or replacement does not cancel the unpaid agreed minimum; confirmed payment credits reduce it. There is no additional replacement fee or automatic refund. You may seek help or replacement at any time. Safety or teaching non-delivery receives independent staff review and can lead to an adjusted minimum; no one must continue unsafe teaching to request review. The platform's manual credit record is not bank settlement, and a refund assessment is not a refund sent.

**हिन्दी:** स्वीकार करने से पहले शिक्षक, विषय, सहमत मासिक फ़ीस और0/15/30 दिन की न्यूनतम फ़ीस देखें। डेमो वैकल्पिक है।15 दिन की राशि सहमत मासिक फ़ीस की आधी और30 दिन की राशि एक महीने की है। न्यूनतम फ़ीस तभी शुरू होती है जब पैसों के अधिकृत दोनों पक्ष पढ़ाई शुरू होने की एक ही वास्तविक तारीख की पुष्टि करें। सामान्य रूप से जल्दी छोड़ने या शिक्षक बदलने से बकाया न्यूनतम फ़ीस नहीं मिटती; दोनों से पुष्ट भुगतान उसमें घटता है। शिक्षक बदलने का अतिरिक्त शुल्क या अपने-आप रिफ़ंड नहीं है। कभी भी मदद या बदलाव माँग सकते हैं। सुरक्षा या पढ़ाई न कराने की शिकायत की स्वतंत्र कर्मचारी समीक्षा होती है और न्यूनतम राशि घट सकती है। समीक्षा माँगने के लिए असुरक्षित पढ़ाई जारी रखना ज़रूरी नहीं है। हाथ से दर्ज भुगतान बैंक का निपटान नहीं है और रिफ़ंड की समीक्षा का मतलब पैसे लौटाना नहीं है।
